# Global_Airbnb_Performance_Dashboard
### Airbnb Data Analysis – Key Insights

This project analyzes Airbnb listing data from 2008 to 2021 to understand listing growth, market distribution, property pricing, and customer ratings across different cities.
### 1. Airbnb Dataset Overview
The analysis reveals the following key statistics:

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Total Property Listings | Approximately 279.7K |
| Total Cities | 10 |
| Total Hosts | Approximately 182.02K |
| Types of Properties | 144 |
| Total Reviews | Approximately 5.37 Million |

### 2. Airbnb Growth Trend (2008–2021)
The analysis of Airbnb's growth over time highlights the following trends:

- 2008 – Establishment: Airbnb was established in 2008 and entered the market.

- 2008–2011 – Initial Stage: Airbnb experienced relatively slow growth during its early years.

- 2011–2012 – Growth Phase: The platform started gaining popularity, resulting in an increase in property listings.

- 2015 – Peak: Airbnb experienced significant growth and reached its peak in terms of listings.

- 2016–2017 – Decline: A decline in listings was observed, potentially influenced by changes in local regulations and policies.

- 2018 – Stabilization: The market started stabilizing after the previous decline.

- 2019 – Recovery: Airbnb showed signs of renewed growth.

- 2019–2020 – COVID-19 Impact: The travel and hospitality industry experienced a significant downturn due to the COVID-19 pandemic, affecting Airbnb's business.

### 3. City-Wise Market Share Analysis
- Paris and New York are the top two cities in terms of property listings, reflecting their popularity as major tourist destinations.

- These cities represent a significant share of the Airbnb market among the 10 cities analyzed.

### 4. Average Property Prices by Room Type
The analysis highlights differences in average prices across property types:

| Property Type | Average Price |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel | $800 |
| Entire Place | $673 |
| Shared Room | $580 |
| Private Room | $462 |

Key Finding: Hotels have the highest average price ($800), while private rooms have the lowest average price ($462).

### 5. City-Wise Average Ratings

Based on the overall average ratings:

1. Mexico ranks first.

2. Rio de Janeiro ranks second.

3. Cape Town ranks third.

These cities demonstrate strong customer satisfaction based on the average ratings in the dataset.

### 6. Category-Wise Customer Ratings

- Mexico and Rio de Janeiro perform particularly well across multiple rating categories.

- Both cities receive high ratings in categories such as:

- Accuracy

- Communication

- Location

Key Finding: Mexico and Rio de Janeiro stand out for their strong performance across multiple customer rating categories, indicating positive guest experiences.

### Conclusion
The Airbnb analysis provides insights into the platform's growth, market distribution, property pricing, and customer satisfaction across 10 cities.

The key findings indicate that Paris and New York dominate the listing market, hotels have the highest average property prices, and Mexico and Rio de Janeiro perform strongly in customer ratings.

This analysis helps understand Airbnb's market trends and provides a data-driven perspective on the global short-term rental market.
